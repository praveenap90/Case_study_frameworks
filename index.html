Case interview numbers and frameworks

What this page is about

A case interview gives you a business problem and asks you to think out loud and do quick math. It is common for data analyst and data engineer roles. A framework is just a simple structure for organizing your thinking, so you do not freeze or miss something important.

This page is a study kit in four parts: pick the approach, know the question types, sharpen your math, then practice and review. Every section follows the same order: the idea, a worked example, practice questions, pictures and flash cards.

How to use this page
  1. Open one section. Read the concept and the worked example.
  2. Try the practice questions before you tap the answer.
  3. Tap a flash card to flip it. Say the answer out loud first.
  4. Tap any highlighted term, like BCG matrix or Payback, to see what it means. Mark a section as done when you finish it.
